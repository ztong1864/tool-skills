# Linker And Domain-Communication Sites

## Scope

This page covers linker insertions, split-domain effects, and CTD-side tRNA-binding positions that modulate communication between NTD, CTD, tRNA(Pyl), and substrate.

| Feature | Scaffold | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| P149/A150 junction | MmPylRS | Linker insertion site in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md). |
| D1 stop/start spacer | MmPylRS and N-PylRS | Tests whether NTD/CTD linkage can be interrupted and reassembled in cells in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md). |
| L1/L2/L3 GGGGS-type linkers | MmPylRS and ZRS variants | Moderate-length linker effects can improve or alter suppression depending on scaffold in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md). |
| split PylRS around Met107 | chPylRS | Evolved split architectures in [S03](../sources/S03-pace-aaRS-2017/summary.md) and [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md). |
| S193R | MmPylRS | CTD-side tRNA-binding-region mutation in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md) and part of Com1-IFRS in [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md). |

## Recommendation Use

Linker changes are not ordinary single-site mutations. For single-site recommendation tasks, use this page mainly to infer physical state and mechanism, not as a direct candidate source unless the task explicitly allows insertions or domain architecture edits.
