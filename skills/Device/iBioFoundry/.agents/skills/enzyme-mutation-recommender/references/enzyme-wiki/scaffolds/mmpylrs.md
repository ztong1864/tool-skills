# MmPylRS And Mm-Derived Variants

## Reference Scaffold

MmPylRS refers to Methanosarcina mazei pyrrolysyl-tRNA synthetase. The user-provided wild-type sequence in future task boards is expected to be treated as the primary MmPylRS-like reference unless a task states otherwise.

## Domain Features From Sources

- The enzyme has an N-terminal/tRNA-binding region, a linker, and a C-terminal catalytic domain. See [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md) and [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md).
- The NTD contacts the T-loop and minimal variable loop of tRNA(Pyl), while the CTD contains the substrate activation site and also contacts tRNA(Pyl). See [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md).
- Linker geometry between NTD and CTD can alter suppression efficiency and substrate range. See [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md).

## Variant Families

- MmPylRS wild-type: source scaffold for structural interpretation and NTD evolution.
- MmPylRS(N346A/C348A): polysubstrate Phe-analog pocket variant compared with MbPylRS(N311A/C313A) in [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md).
- R3-11 MmPylRS: R19H/H29R/T122S on MmPylRS, evolved for BocK in [S05](../sources/S05-evolving-ntd-2018/summary.md).
- N-PylRS: R61K/H63Y/S193R on MmPylRS, used for substrate range shifting in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md).
- IFRS: MmPylRS-derived N346I/C348S variant for halogenated Phe analogs, used in [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md).

## Interpretation Notes

- Mm numbering is not automatically equivalent to Mb numbering. Use homolog statements from source papers or explicit sequence alignment.
- NTD/TBD mutations can improve, reduce, or fail to affect activity depending on catalytic pocket and substrate.
- For recommendations on Mm-like sequences, prioritize Mm-derived and IFRS-derived evidence before Mb-only evidence unless a site can be confidently mapped.
