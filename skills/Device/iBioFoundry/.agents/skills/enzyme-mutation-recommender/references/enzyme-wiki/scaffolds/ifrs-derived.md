# IFRS-Derived Variants

## Definition

IFRS is an MmPylRS-derived catalytic-pocket variant reported as N346I/C348S and selected for 3-iodo-Phe. It is a key scaffold for halogenated phenylalanine analog evidence.

## Source

Primary source: [S07 - ML-guided PylRS evolution](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md).

## Evidence Summary

- IFRS accepts 3-iodo-Phe and has broad substrate range.
- 3-bromo-Phe was used as a cheaper proxy substrate for optimization.
- Some NTD/TBD mutation sets beneficial in other scaffolds failed in IFRS.
- D2N/K3N/T56P/H62Y improved IFRS SCS efficiency by about sevenfold.
- Com1-IFRS improved SCS efficiency about elevenfold.
- Com2-IFRS improved SCS efficiency about 30.8-fold and catalytic efficiency up to 7.8-fold.

## Interpretation Notes

- IFRS is the most relevant source family in this Wiki for halogenated Phe analog activity, but it is still L-Phe analog evidence unless a task supplies D-form data.
- Single-site recommendations from IFRS must account for epistasis; individually weak or harmful changes can be beneficial in combinations, and individually beneficial changes can conflict.
