# Mechanism Map

This page summarizes mechanism families. Use the specific mechanism pages and source summaries for evidence details.

## Mechanism Families

1. tRNA recognition through NTD/TBD geometry
   - PylRS NTD recognizes unusual tRNA(Pyl) T-loop and minimal variable loop features.
   - Anticodon independence contributes to orthogonality and recoding flexibility.
   - Sources: [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md), [S02](../sources/S02-chimeric-aaRS-tRNA-2020/summary.md).

2. NTD/CTD/tRNA communication
   - NTD and CTD bind opposite sides of tRNA(Pyl), so changes remote from the active site can change aminoacylation efficiency.
   - Sources: [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md), [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md), [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md).

3. Substrate-pocket coupling
   - Active-site mutations set substrate scope, but tRNA-binding-domain mutations can alter apparent substrate activity by changing charging efficiency and domain geometry.
   - Sources: [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md), [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md), [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md).

4. Epistasis and scaffold dependence
   - The same single mutation or mutation set can be beneficial, neutral, or harmful depending on scaffold and co-mutations.
   - Sources: [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md), [S03](../sources/S03-pace-aaRS-2017/summary.md), [S05](../sources/S05-evolving-ntd-2018/summary.md).

5. Assay and reporter effects
   - Fluorescence and expression reporters can be distorted by protein folding, reporter position, substrate concentration, and product chemistry.
   - Sources: [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md), [S08](../sources/S08-two-tier-screening-2017/summary.md).
