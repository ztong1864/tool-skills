# Substrate Classes

## Aromatic Phenylalanine Analogs

- Meta-substituted L-Phe analogs, including 3-iodo-L-Phe, 3-bromo-L-Phe, 3-methyl-L-Phe, and 3-chloro-L-Phe, are tested in [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md).
- IFRS is selected for 3-iodo-Phe and optimized using 3-bromo-Phe in [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md).
- Chimeric PheRS systems incorporate Phe/Tyr/Trp analogs in [S02](../sources/S02-chimeric-aaRS-tRNA-2020/summary.md), but that evidence belongs to chimeric canonical-aaRS systems, not directly to PylRS active-site variants.

## Lysine And Acyl-Lysine Analogs

- BocK appears in [S03](../sources/S03-pace-aaRS-2017/summary.md), [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md), [S05](../sources/S05-evolving-ntd-2018/summary.md), and [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md).
- AcK and AcdK transfer tests appear in [S05](../sources/S05-evolving-ntd-2018/summary.md).
- CrtK, BuK, PrK, AcK, and AynK appear in [S08](../sources/S08-two-tier-screening-2017/summary.md).
- CbzK and related lysine analogs appear in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md).

## Cysteine And Histidine Analogs

- S-benzyl cysteine analogs and N-pi-methyl-L-histidine are enabled by N-PylRS in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md).

## D-Form Substrates

- [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md) includes D-CbzK-like lysine analog tests, with scaffold-specific loss or change of activity.
- The current initial corpus does not provide direct evidence for D-3BrF, D-3CF3F, or related large/halogenated D-Phe substrates. Recommendations for those substrates must be labeled as extrapolation unless future task data or literature is added.
