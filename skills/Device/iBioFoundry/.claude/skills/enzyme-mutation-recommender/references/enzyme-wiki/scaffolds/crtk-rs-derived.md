# CrtK-RS And Lysine-Acyl Variants

## Definition

CrtK-RS is an MbPylRS-derived synthetase for N-epsilon-crotonyl-lysine. It is a core scaffold for NTD remote-mutation evidence but not a direct phenylalanine-analog scaffold.

## Source

Primary source: [S08 - Two-tier screening platform](../sources/S08-two-tier-screening-2017/summary.md).

## Evidence Summary

- CrtK-RS(4.1) carries V8E/T13I/I36V/H45L/S121R/I355T.
- Most beneficial substitutions are in the N-terminal noncatalytic domain; I355T is CTD but remote from substrate in the homologous model.
- CrtK-RS(4.1) improved CrtK incorporation by about 250-370% depending on assay.
- The mutation set transferred to AynK-RS with about 2.2-fold improvement.

## Interpretation Notes

- Strong evidence for remote NTD/TBD activity enhancement.
- Weak direct evidence for Phe analog or D-amino-acid recommendation unless a mechanism is mapped and the user scaffold matches relevant structural criteria.
