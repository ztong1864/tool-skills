# MbPylRS And Mb-Derived Variants

## Reference Scaffold

MbPylRS refers to Methanosarcina barkeri pyrrolysyl-tRNA synthetase. It shares substantial identity with MmPylRS but differs enough that NTD/TBD effects cannot be assumed to transfer cleanly.

## Variant Families

- MbPylRS(N311A/C313A), also called Mb-NA/CA in [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md), is homologous to MmPylRS(N346A/C348A) and showed higher reporter activity for 12 tested Phe analogs.
- IPE: MbPylRS(N311A/C313A/V31I/T56P/A100E), a greedy-combined polysubstrate variant in [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md).
- CrtK-RS and AynK-RS are MbPylRS-derived lysine-acyl synthetases covered separately in [CrtK-RS derived scaffolds](crtk-rs-derived.md).

## Interpretation Notes

- Mb N311/C313 are treated in [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md) as corresponding to Mm N346/C348. Other positions require alignment.
- [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md) explicitly warns that an NTD mutation set beneficial in Mm may have negligible benefit in Mb, so transfer claims require source support.
- IPE is strong evidence for Mb-NA/CA and Phe-analog polysubstrate use; it is not automatically strong evidence for Mm IFRS or D-amino-acid substrates.
