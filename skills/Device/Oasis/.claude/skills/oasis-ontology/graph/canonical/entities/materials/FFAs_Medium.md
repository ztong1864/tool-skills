# Material: FFAs Medium

id: material:fm-ffas-medium
name: FM / FFAs Medium

FM means `FFAs Medium`.

This material is a culture medium containing free fatty acids (FFAs).

In the BODIPY lipid staining experiment, adding FFAs is used to simulate the elevated blood lipid condition associated with fatty liver disease models.

In plate layout interpretation:

- `FM` corresponds to the FFAs medium group
- this group is used as the comparison threshold group in downstream BODIPY analysis
