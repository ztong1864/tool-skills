# Chimeric PylRS Scaffolds

## Definition

Chimeric PylRS scaffolds combine domains from different Methanosarcina PylRS origins or fuse PylRS tRNA-binding domains to non-PylRS catalytic domains.

## Key Sources

- [S03](../sources/S03-pace-aaRS-2017/summary.md): chPylRS with MbPylRS residues 1-149 and MmPylRS residues 185-454 evolved by PACE.
- [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md): chPylRS and evolved variants are used for structural and kinetic interpretation.
- [S02](../sources/S02-chimeric-aaRS-tRNA-2020/summary.md): PylRS TD is transplanted into canonical aaRS/tRNA systems.

## Important Variants

- chPylRS IPYE: V31I/T56P/H62Y/A100E in the chimeric PylRS context, associated with improved BocK incorporation and transfer to several PylRS-derived synthetases.
- 32A NTD: D2N/K3N/T56P/H62Y in the structural source, used to understand altered NTD-tRNA contacts.
- Split PylRS variants: evolved frameshift/reinitiation architectures that preserve activity in some contexts.

## Interpretation Notes

- Chimeric numbering follows the construct. Do not assume a chimeric position is identical to full-length Mm or Mb without domain-boundary mapping.
- Chimeric sources are powerful for mechanism extraction but are medium-confidence for direct single-site recommendation in non-chimeric scaffolds unless transfer is demonstrated.
