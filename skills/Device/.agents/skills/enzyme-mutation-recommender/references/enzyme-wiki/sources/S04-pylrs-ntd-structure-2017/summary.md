# S04 - PylRS NTD Structure, 2017

PDF: [paper.pdf](paper.pdf)

Original title: "Crystal structures reveal an elusive functional domain of pyrrolysyl-tRNA synthetase"

## Scope

This paper provides the central structural evidence for PylRS N-terminal-domain recognition of tRNA(Pyl), explains anticodon independence and orthogonality, and interprets evolved NTD mutations.

## Scaffold And Numbering

- Structural scaffold: M. mazei PylRS N-terminal domain bound to M. mazei tRNA(Pyl).
- Evolved comparison: 32A NTD carrying D2N/K3N/T56P/H62Y in the NTD context.
- Related evolved full enzymes include chPylRS variants such as 32A, 24B, and 25C.
- Numbering is M. mazei/chimeric-context numbering as stated in the source; do not merge with Mb numbering without explicit alignment.

## Assays And Substrates

- X-ray crystallography of wild-type NTD-tRNA(Pyl) and evolved NTD-tRNA(Pyl) complexes.
- PANCE evolution and sfGFP readthrough assays.
- Kinetic assays measured aminoacylation parameters for Pyl and BocK with tRNA(Pyl).

## Mutation Evidence

- Wild-type NTD forms extensive contacts with the T-loop and minimal variable loop of tRNA(Pyl).
- In the evolved 32A NTD, K3N disrupts hydrogen bonds to the tRNA T-loop.
- H62Y disrupts additional hydrogen bonds but establishes new weaker contacts with G21 phosphate and A20 base.
- NTD-only evolved mutations can weaken apparent NTD-tRNA affinity; full evolved variants recover or improve activity through additional CTD changes.

## Mechanistic Evidence

- Direct evidence: the NTD fits into the concave tRNA(Pyl) surface formed by T-loop and variable loop; canonical tRNA variable arms would sterically clash.
- Direct evidence: the tRNA anticodon does not contact PylRS in the structural model, explaining anticodon independence.
- Direct evidence: kinetic data show evolved variants can improve BocK affinity and tRNA-related catalytic efficiency while reducing Pyl affinity.
- Cross-paper synthesis: beneficial engineering can shift the burden of tRNA recognition between NTD and CTD, making tRNA binding and amino acid activation interdependent.

## Caveats

- Structural evidence is strongest for the NTD-tRNA interface and BocK/Pyl kinetic context.
- The evolved NTD alone may weaken tRNA affinity; full activity depends on the complete enzyme background.
