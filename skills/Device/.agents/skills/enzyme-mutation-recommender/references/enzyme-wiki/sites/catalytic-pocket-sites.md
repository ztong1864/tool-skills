# Catalytic-Pocket Sites

## Scope

This page records substrate-pocket positions from the initial corpus. These positions are often directly tied to substrate class and must be interpreted with scaffold context.

| Site | Source scaffold | Source numbering | Evidence |
|---|---|---:|---|
| N346/C348 | MmPylRS | Mm numbering | MmPylRS(N346A/C348A) accepts many Phe derivatives but lower efficiency than homologous Mb variant in [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md). |
| N311/C313 | MbPylRS | Mb numbering | MbPylRS(N311A/C313A) has higher activity than Mm N346A/C348A across 12 Phe analogs in [S01](../sources/S01-polysubstrate-pylrs-2023/summary.md). |
| N346/C348 as N346I/C348S | IFRS/MmPylRS-derived | Mm numbering | IFRS selected for 3-iodo-Phe and used for 3BrF optimization in [S07](../sources/S07-ml-guided-pylrs-2025/summary.md). |
| Y349 and CTD contact sites | chPylRS/Mm CTD context | source numbering | CTD changes can affect substrate and tRNA binding in [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md). |

## Recommendation Use

Active-site positions are strong substrate-specific evidence but can change substrate selectivity. When the user's request asks only for single-site recommendations on an existing substrate pocket, treat additional active-site changes as higher-risk than remote NTD/TBD changes unless the Wiki has direct substrate evidence.
