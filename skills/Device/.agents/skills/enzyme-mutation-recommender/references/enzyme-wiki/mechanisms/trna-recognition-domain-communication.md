# tRNA Recognition And Domain Communication

## Direct Evidence

- [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md) shows that the MmPylRS NTD fits into a tRNA(Pyl) surface formed by the T-loop and minimal variable loop.
- [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md) reports polar contacts between NTD residues and tRNA(Pyl), including K3 and H24-related contacts in the wild-type structure.
- [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md) shows that the anticodon does not contact the enzyme in the observed recognition mode.
- [S04](../sources/S04-pylrs-ntd-structure-2017/summary.md) compares evolved 32A NTD with wild-type NTD and finds that K3N and H62Y disrupt or remodel tRNA contacts.

## Cross-Paper Synthesis

- NTD mutations can improve in-cell suppression even when they weaken some local NTD-tRNA contacts, because the complete enzyme can compensate through CTD-side tRNA binding and substrate-positioning changes.
- Linker and NTD/CTD boundary changes in [S06](../sources/S06-linker-ntd-engineering-2020/summary.md) support the idea that the tRNA-bound enzyme behaves as a coupled architecture rather than two independent domains.
- Chimeric designs in [S02](../sources/S02-chimeric-aaRS-tRNA-2020/summary.md) show that a PylRS tRNA-binding module can confer useful orthogonality and suppression behavior outside native PylRS catalytic context.

## Recommendation Implication

For a user sequence, infer whether existing mutations already alter tRNA recognition or domain communication. New remote-site recommendations should be justified by a Wiki-derived mechanism and by scaffold similarity, not by a generic belief that NTD mutations are good.
