 General Information.  NMR spectra were recorded with a Bruker AM-300 or
AM-400 instrument. Chemical shifts are reported in relative to TMS [1H: δ = 0.00
ppm (TMS)], solvent residual peaks [13C: δ = 77.0 ppm (CDCl 3)] or internal standard
[19F: δ = 0.00 ppm (CFCl 3)]. IR spectra were measured  with a Bruker Tensor 27
instrument. MS analyses were performe d with an Aglient Technologies 5975C
instrument. HRMS analyses were performe d with a Waters GCT Premier instrument.
All reactions were carried out in  oven dried Schlenk tubes. ZnBr 2 was purchased from
Alfa Aesar (98%) and kept in a glove box;  CuBr was purchased from Acros (98%),
1-dodecyne 1a (97%), 1-decyne 1b (98%), 1-octyne 1c (98%), and
5-chloro-1-pentyne 1d (98%) were purchased from Alfa Aesar;
(S)-α,α-diphenylprolinol 3 (98%)  was purchased from Shanghai  Darui Fine Chemicals.
Liquid aldehydes were distilled right before use. Toluene was dried over sodium wire
with benzophenone as the indicator and dis tilled freshly before use. Other reagents
were used as received from commercial sources. NMR yield was determined by using
CH 2Br2 as the  internal standard. Petroleum ether (60 ℃-90℃) was used for
chromatography. All the temperatures are referred to the oil baths used.
