In conclusion, we have developed a general allenylation
of terminal alkynols with aliphatic or aromatic aldehydesusing ( S)-α,α-dimethylprolinol instead of ( S)-α,α-diphenyl-
prolinol, affording a series of optically active 1,3-disubsti-tuted allenols with high enantioselectivity in one-pot. Thesynthetic potentials of these allenols prepared have alsobeen demonstrated by oxidation to aldehyde and conver-sion to amide, as well as a different approach for the natu-
rally occurring phlomic acid.
